### PR Description

Support `EPBuffer` resizing, FP32 combine epilogue output, relaxed hidden alignment in combine, redundant-expert mapping generation (`lb_get_redundancy_mapping`) with scale-out support, and synthetic top-k routing generation.

Restore bucket `send_recv` across NVLink, RDMA, and hybrid domains, add CP `ring_send_recv`, support explicit target accumulation in `reduce_scatter`, eliminate register spills in NVLink `all_reduce`, and add CUDA Graph capture support for copy-engine collectives.

Migrate CUDA driver wrappers to DeepJIT and update the DeepJIT submodule to `18fe520`.

Squash 22 Nightly commits relative to public main (excluding c88d141a). Source: deepseek-ai/DeepEP-Nightly@fdc9cc36aa0bdd03b4731d8cb22ca93ceaa1a8c7
