### PR Description

Remove the HCCL runtime dependency from HCOMM bootstrap, topology discovery, and UBMEM channels in favor of PyTorch `ProcessGroup` and `topoaddrinfo`. Add AICPU execution support, hybrid scale-up/scale-out barriers, recoverable error reporting, and host-wait abort recovery.

Expand Bucket collectives with deterministic FP32 `all_reduce`, scale-up and scale-out `reduce_scatter`, scale-out and hybrid `all_gather`, ACLGraph capture support for AIV `all_gather`, general `send_recv`, CP `ring_send_recv`, and stand-by buffer takeover.

Support `EPBuffer` resizing, FP32 combine output, redundant-expert load balancing (`lb_get_redundancy_mapping`, `lb_prefetch_weights`, and `lb_reduce_grads`), and synthetic top-k routing generation. Update the DeepJIT submodule to `b1221a1`.

Squash 44 Nightly commits relative to public main. Source: deepseek-ai/DeepEP-Ascend-Nightly@563e44623d607727dff8db34024f89334d317d71
